# College Student Financial Preparedness

## Project Overview

This project investigates which financial habits and educational experiences are associated with emergency savings among young adults aged 18–24.

Using data from the 2024 National Financial Capability Study (NFCS), the goal is to identify patterns associated with having enough emergency savings to cover three months of expenses.

## Research Question

Which financial habits and educational experiences are most strongly associated with having sufficient emergency savings among young adults aged 18–24?

## Machine Learning Approach

- **Problem type:** Binary classification
- **Target variable:** `J5` – Emergency savings sufficient to cover three months of expenses
- **Initial model:** Logistic Regression

## Planned Features

- `M20` – Financial education participation
- `B43` – Use of budgeting and financial apps
- `J3` – Spending relative to income
- `F2_1` – Credit-card payment behavior
- `M4` – Self-assessed financial knowledge
